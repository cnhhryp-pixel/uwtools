How BMI is calculated

Metric BMI = kilograms ÷ meters². Imperial BMI uses pounds and inches with the standard conversion factor.

What BMI can and cannot tell you

BMI is a screening ratio, not a diagnosis. It does not directly measure body fat, muscle mass or overall health.
