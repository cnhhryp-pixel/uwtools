WCAG contrast thresholds

For normal text, WCAG AA requires at least 4.5:1 and AAA requires 7:1. Large text uses lower thresholds of 3:1 for AA and 4.5:1 for AAA.

What the ratio means

A higher contrast ratio generally improves readability between foreground text and the background.
