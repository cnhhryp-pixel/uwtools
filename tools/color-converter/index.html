HEX, RGB and HSL conversion

HEX is compact for CSS, RGB expresses red, green and blue channels, and HSL represents hue, saturation and lightness.

Accepted formats

HEX accepts 3- or 6-digit values. RGB accepts values such as 37, 99, 235 or rgb(37,99,235). HSL accepts values such as 221, 83%, 53% or hsl(221,83%,53%).
