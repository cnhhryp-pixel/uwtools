What are UTM parameters?

UTM parameters are query-string labels added to a URL so analytics platforms can attribute visits to a campaign source, medium and campaign name.

Required fields

Source, medium and campaign are the most useful core fields. Term and content are optional and help separate keywords, creatives or placements.

Tip

Use consistent lowercase naming to keep analytics reports easier to group.
